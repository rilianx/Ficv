Sinopsis

Simón vive en un pueblo abandonado. Allí pasa una última noche junto a su hermana mayor, Sol, quien se irá a trabajar a la ciudad. Junto con un grupo de amigxs, recorren los oscuros caminos del lugar donde crecieron, iluminando las ruinas que van cobrando vida con el paso de sus linternas.

Synopsis

Simón lives in an abandoned village. There, he spends one last night with his older sister, Sol, who is leaving to find work in the city. Together with a group of friends, they wander the dark paths of the place where they grew up, illuminating the ruins that come to life in the glow of their flashlights.
