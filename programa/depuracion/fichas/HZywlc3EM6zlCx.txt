Sinopsis

En su séptimo cumpleaños, un niño amante de las aves recibe un cardenalito como regalo. Al descubrir que el ave es padre y que sus hijos han quedado abandonados, deciden embarcarse en una peligrosa aventura para que pueda volver con su familia.

Synopsis

On his seventh birthday, a boy who loves birds receives a cardinal as a gift. When he discovers that the bird is a father and that his chicks have been left behind, they decide to embark on a dangerous adventure so that he can return to his family.
